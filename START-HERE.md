# Start here — UMSKAL floor gallery edition

Read FLOOR-GALLERY-GUIDE.md for the new left-side floor buttons, swipeable galleries and photo configuration.

Extract Labuan-Ar-Floor-Gallery.zip into XAMPP htdocs and open http://localhost/Labuan-Ar-Floor-Gallery/ on your computer. Use your published HTTPS address for the phone camera.

Choose AR and scan UMSKAL: the floor directory appears automatically. Or choose Explore in 3D first to try the same gallery without a camera.

Edit coding/umskal-floor-gallery.js to add real photographs for each floor. Existing sample floor descriptions live in coding/content.js. The included images are labelled sample illustrations, not real UMSKAL photos.

UPDATE-GUIDE.md covers the previous tabletop, narration, Story video and four-location setup. Those features remain available. Only UMSKAL Discover was redesigned in this update.
