# Start here — tabletop AR edition

Read UPDATE-GUIDE.md for the five changes, media slots and presentation setup.

1. Extract Labuan-Ar-Tabletop.zip.
2. Copy the extracted Labuan-Ar-Tabletop folder into XAMPP's htdocs.
3. Start Apache and open http://localhost/Labuan-Ar-Tabletop/ on that computer.
4. Choose Explore in 3D first to switch between UMSKAL, Clock Tower, Surrender Point and LISC.
5. For a phone camera demonstration, use your published HTTPS website address. Keep index.html, assets, coding and libs together at the website root.
6. Place the UMSKAL or Clock Tower marker flat on your map, open AR, and allow the camera. Tap Explore & listen to open the controls over the full-screen camera.

Surrender Point and LISC are ready in the 3D preview. Their AR marker slots are empty until you supply matching compiled markers. All four locations have story video slots; only UMSKAL currently has narration files.

UMSKAL floor information remains clearly labelled sample content and the floor bands are approximate. The existing long narration is separate from those sample floor descriptions. Original assets are preserved.

The browser may require a tap for sound or retain its browser bars. Test the published site on the actual presentation phone with your printed map and volume enabled.

The passport supports all four locations and saves progress in the current browser. Completing every discovery for a location OR answering its challenge correctly earns its stamp. It records exploration, not a verified physical visit.
